# ADR 0032: Starting rooms, close-range camera fixes and flush back weapons

Date: 2026-11-14. **Status: accepted for implementation**, at the user's request after reviewing ADR 0031's venue. Human rendered review is pending.

## Decision

**Starting rooms (`arena_sim::arena`).** Each team now spawns in its own room behind a 6 m gateway through the pit wall: friendly (even) actors at +Z, opposing (odd) actors at −Z, 3.5 m inside the room with teammates ±3.5 m apart. Rooms are 14 m wide and 9.5 m deep behind the 1.5 m wall (`GATE_HALF_WIDTH`, `ROOM_HALF_WIDTH`, `ROOM_DEPTH`, `WALL_THICKNESS`). The rules model walkable space as five overlapping rectangles for a body centre (floor, two gateways, two rooms), each already shrunk by the body radius. Each axis moves as far as any region containing the body allows. Gateways overlap the floor and rooms by 1 m, more than one tick of movement, so bodies cross between them without ever leaving all regions. Wall faces beside each gateway, the gateway sides and each room's front wall block line of sight, so a room is visible only through its gateway. There are no doors or countdown yet; the gateways are always open. The spawn gap grows from 27 m to about 94 m, so an opening approach at 7.2 m/s takes about 12 s.

**Venue.** `venue.rs` carves each gateway (4 m clear) through the pit wall and hollows each room out of the lowest five stand tiers: their sides become the room walls and the part above (from 5 m) its ceiling, so the room is enclosed by the same solid tiers. Inside are a flagstone floor, brick-faced walls and a plank ceiling on wooden beams. Each portcullis is raised into the lintel, with only its lower edge showing, behind stone jambs and a gilded lintel. A test checks that the carved interiors match the rules' walkable rooms.

**Flat teal strips removed.** The untextured teal marker strips on the pillars and above the gates read as plastic and are gone; every venue surface is now textured.

**Camera.**

- *Inside the character.* With the character close to a wall and the camera pushed in, the eye retracted to 0.15–0.5 m from the chest pivot, inside the character's own body, so the animated mesh filled and shook the view. The player's model is now hidden while the eye is within 1.1 m of the pivot.
- *Dragged beside the head.* When a pillar or wall right beside the player lay between it and the requested eye, the eye was dragged along that face off the boom, so the view swung as the player moved, then popped around corners. Any obstacle entered within 2 m of the pivot now always shortens the boom, keeping the eye on it.
- *Occlusion only by pillars.* ADR 0014 lets a distant pillar sit between the player and the camera. That permission now applies only to free-standing boxes (pillars). Floors, walls, stands, ceilings and the building always retract the boom, so the camera cannot wedge behind room ceilings or stand steps. ADR 0031's straight-to-target rule is narrowed: an eye that makes no progress for a frame rejoins the boom at no more than its current distance, removing the up-to-10 m pops it could cause.

**Flush back weapons.** Mounts were set 0.36–0.40 m behind the body so they cleared BaseFemale's braid, which hangs down the centre of the upper back to 0.27 m, leaving 7–14 cm gaps at the torso. Each weapon group is now fitted per body (`weapons::back_fit`): an inward shift and a lean about the blades' crossing point, so the weapon rests 1.5 cm from its nearest surface. The fit was solved numerically against the imported glaives and staff and the procedural fallback weapons, using the procedural fallback's parts and BaseFemale's rest mesh. BaseFemale's weapons lean 11° (glaives) and 8.5° (staff) outward over the braid while resting on the torso. Her staff sits about 4.7 cm off the torso because of the braid; every other combination is 1.5 cm. BaseMale has no local source to measure and keeps its reviewed placement. The fit follows whichever body is visible: the loaded model once ready, otherwise the fallback. Weapons stay on the motion carrier, not a chest bone, so authored poses that lean back may still bring the torso closer.

## Verification

New simulation tests cover spawning in rooms for every team size, leaving through a gateway and crossing into the other room, gateway sides, room front, side and back walls, solid pit wall beside the gateways, and sight through gateways but not walls. Existing tests now use positions beside the gateway where they test the pit wall. Client tests check that room interiors and gateways are open while their walls, lintel and ceiling are solid, that the carved rooms match the rules and that no flat strips remain. The per-body weapon fits and their transforms are tested, the camera orbit regression now includes both rooms and a gateway and requires the eye to settle on its boom, and a new camera test walks past a pillar corner checking for sideways pops and holds still against a pillar and a wall checking the eye does not move. A temporary probe reproduced the inside-the-body and dragged-eye cases before the fix.

Local verification on 2026-11-14: `./scripts/check.sh` passed (88 client, 14 simulation, 6 texture-tool, 28 Node input and 166 Vitest tests, plus Python, Clippy, format and frontend checks). WASM Clippy with warnings denied, `./scripts/build-web.sh` with its static check (15 resources) and the headless scenario passed. No browser was launched.

Human review should check: spawning in the rooms and walking out through each gateway, the room interiors and raised portcullises, camera feel inside rooms, in gateways and pressed against walls and pillars, the player model hiding up close, and both weapons on both bodies at rest, walking and jumping, from the side.
