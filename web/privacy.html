Privacy notice

Logging in is optional. Guests can play without an account; the game keeps a random guest id in your browser so a party stays together.

Operators: add who runs this server and how to reach them before offering login.

Who holds what

  • WorkOS runs the login page. It holds your email address and your login methods (password, emailed codes, or the Google or GitHub account you log in with). The game never sees your password.
  • This game stores only your WorkOS user id, the name you choose (Name#1234), when you made the account, logged in and last changed your name, and your friends, friend requests and blocks. It stores no email address, password or payment details.
  • Chat messages pass through the server to the players who hear them and are not stored.
  • The web server keeps access logs, which include your IP address. The game page also sends scrubbed error and performance reports (crashes, graphics errors, load time and one network summary per match), which are kept in the server logs. They carry no account, name or chat. Turn them off by opening the game with ?telemetry=off in the address, or with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control enabled in your browser.
  • Your login is kept in a cookie that only this site can read, for up to 30 days or until you log out.

Deleting your account

Open Play online on the home screen or in the game menu while logged in and choose Delete account. This deletes your WorkOS user, your name, your friends, requests and blocks at once and ends all your sessions. If WorkOS cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and you can try again.
